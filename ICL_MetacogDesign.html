Invitation to Participate

You are invited to participate in this study to help us understand how designers generate ideas and evaluate the quality of their own work. You will be asked to read a series of short design problems, generate concept ideas for each, and answer questions about your confidence in your own ideas. You will also complete a brief questionnaire about your design background and thinking style. This study is conducted entirely online through Prolific via Qualtrics and JavaScript. To be eligible, you must be 18 years of age or older and fluent in English. This research is funded by the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (NSERC). The researchers have no conflicts of interest to declare.

Voluntary Participation

Participation in this study is voluntary and will take approximately 30 minutes of your time. In appreciation of your time, you will be compensated approximately £3.70 for your time (which is within the recommended £6.00/hour on Prolific). You may withdraw at any time without penalty, and you may decline to answer any question or complete any part of the study without consequence. You may withdraw by closing the study before submitting your responses; incomplete responses will not be recorded or used.

Risks and Benefits

The risks associated with participation in the study are expected to be no greater than what you might experience in your day-to-day life. There are no direct personal benefits to participation; however, the data collected will help researchers better understand how people generate and evaluate their own design ideas. You may decide to withdraw from this study at any time by clicking through to the end without answering, without any penalty or loss of participation credit. By taking part, you will also gain first-hand experience with research and receive a detailed debriefing about the study's purpose upon completion.

Privacy and Confidentiality

You will be completing this study through Prolific and Qualtrics. When information is transmitted over the internet, privacy cannot be guaranteed. There is always a risk your responses may be intercepted by a third party (e.g., government agencies, hackers). If you prefer not to submit your survey responses through the online platform, please do not participate in this study.

University of Toronto researchers will not collect or use IP addresses or other information which could link your participation to your computer or electronic device without first informing you. Once data has been collected, please note that researchers will have no way of knowing which data belongs to which participant due to the de-identification of data. Your identity will be confidential. Furthermore, because the interest of this study is in the average responses of the entire group of participants, you will not be identified individually in any way in any written reports of this research. De-identified data will be securely stored on the University of Toronto servers, to which only researchers associated with this study have access. De-identified data will also be submitted to an online repository and/or journal (i.e., the data may be publicly available). These data will be completely de-identified prior to submission and will be presented in aggregate form in publications. This process is integral to the research process as it allows other researchers to verify results and avoid duplicating research. Though the data is posted for the purpose listed, other individuals may access these data by accessing the open access repository. Although the dataset without identifiers may be shared publicly, your identity will always remain confidential. Should you not wish to submit your data in this manner, please do not select this study.

Your Prolific ID will be used solely for the purpose of administering compensation and will be stored separately from your study responses.

Written concept descriptions you generate during this study will also be reviewed by independent expert designers as part of the research process. These reviewers will see your design concepts but will not have access to any information that could identify you.
