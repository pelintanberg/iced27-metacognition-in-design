Thank You

Thank you for participating in this study.

The purpose of this study is to examine design calibration: how well a designer's confidence in their own work corresponds to how that work is actually assessed. While you were told the study concerns how people generate and evaluate their own design ideas, we did not initially disclose the specific focus on confidence accuracy, in order to avoid influencing how you approached the confidence rating questions.

Specifically, we asked you to rate your confidence in your ability to generate a satisfactory concept before you began (pre-generation confidence), and your confidence in the actual concept set you produced after generating it (post-generation confidence). Your concepts will also be reviewed by independent expert designers, who will rate how well your concepts meet the stated requirements. By comparing your confidence ratings to these expert assessments, we can examine whether people become better calibrated (i.e., more accurate in judging their own work) once they have direct evidence from their own output, compared to before they've generated anything).

We are also interested in whether your ability to identify your own strongest concept (via the ranking task) relates to how accurately you judge your work overall, and whether these patterns relate to general metacognitive awareness (measured by the questionnaire you completed) and design experience.

If you would like to receive a summary of this study's results once available, you will have the option to provide your email address on the next screen, in a form kept entirely separate from your study responses.

We ask that you do not discuss your experiences with any other individuals who potentially could be in the study. If people participate in the study knowing about our specific predictions it could influence their responses and results and the data that we collect would not be useable.

We appreciate your participation, and hope that this has been an interesting experience for you.
